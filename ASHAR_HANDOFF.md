| ID | Location | Decision | Atomics | Obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-D1 | L58 `if (update_integral)` | branch T/F | — | integral evolves vs frozen |
| A-D2 | L70 `(sat>EPS && err>0) \|\| (sat<-EPS && err<0)` | compound `\|\|` of 2×`&&` | a=`sat>EPS`, b=`err>0`, c=`sat<-EPS`, d=`err<0` | freeze-into-saturation vs allow (incl. unwind) |

  on this, not on assumptions.
- Upstream `PIDTest.cpp` (7 tests) covers: zero-case, output-limit ladder,
